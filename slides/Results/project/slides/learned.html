Findings

What improved accuracy, and what did not

Improved

  • Comparing with healthy plants each day: 0.38 to 0.62 for pixel PLS-DA
  • One vote per plant: 0.61 for single patches to 0.69 per plant
  • Middle ResNet layers: 0.69, against 0.52 for the last layer alone

No change

  • Patches closer together (stride 112, 56, 4): all 0.69 to 0.75
  • Automatic tuning (Bayesian): no better than the defaults
  • Laptop vs Colab GPU: same results, so they are reproducible

Takeaway: the limit is the data, not the settings. Three plants per condition, and low light and shade look alike.

Normalisation removes day-to-day brightness, which was masking the real stress signal. A denser stride covers every region but does not add information that separates low light from shade. Tuning picked different settings for each test plant, a sign there are too few plants to tune reliably.
