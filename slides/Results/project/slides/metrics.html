Appendix

What each term means

TermMeaning
AccuracyShare of plants given the correct condition. 0.25 is random guessing
PrecisionWhen the model names a condition, how often it is right: TP / (TP + FP)
RecallOf the plants truly in a condition, how many it finds: TP / (TP + FN)
F1One score balancing precision and recall, 2PR / (P + R), averaged over the four conditions
TP / FP / FN / TNFor one condition: correctly flagged / wrongly flagged / missed / correctly left out
Confusion matrixActual condition in rows, model’s answer in columns; the diagonal is correct
Plant voteAll squares of a plant vote; the most common label is the plant’s answer
Single patchesEvery square scored on its own, before voting
StrideHow far apart neighbouring squares start; stride 4 covers every region of the plant
White-light normalisationEach image divided by that day’s healthy-plant average, so stress shows as a difference
Stressed vs controlSame answers grouped into two: healthy, or any of the three stresses
Reference slide for questions on the metrics. Precision asks how trustworthy a label is; recall asks how many of the real cases were found; F1 balances the two.
