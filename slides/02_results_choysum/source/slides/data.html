The data

48 three-band images of choy sum

4

Light conditions

White light (healthy control), high light, low light, shade

4

Time points

4 hours, then days 1, 2 and 3

3

Plants

Per condition and time point (P1, P2, P3)

The task: given one image, say which of the four conditions the plant was under. Guessing at random scores 0.25.

Shade was missing from the first batch; it is now included, so this is a four-class problem. Each image is a three-band reflectance image, with the plant separated from the background by a mask.
