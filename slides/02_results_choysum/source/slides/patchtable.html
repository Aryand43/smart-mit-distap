Patch size matters

8 px is too small, 224 px too large; 32 to 64 px works best

Stride matters little

2 vs 4 gives similar accuracy; 4 needs a quarter of the patches

Validation, not test

Scored on the held-out training plant; the test plant is never used

Laptop run, 90 trials over the 3 test plants. Combinations the optimiser never tried (8 × 2, 224 × 2) are not listed

Validation works inside each round: with plant 3 as the test plant, train on plant 1 and check on plant 2, then swap; the validation accuracy is the average of the two checks. The optimiser spends more trials on combinations that look promising, which is why 64 × 4 was tried 53 times. The same table from the GPU run is written to results/10_patch_stride_table.csv.
