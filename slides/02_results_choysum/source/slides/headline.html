Is the plant stressed?

Which light condition?

Correct 69% of the time, against 25% by guessing. High light is always right; low light and shade get mixed up

Best model: ResNet-50 patches (stride 4) + softmax, plant vote, on plants not seen in training

Two numbers to remember. Detecting that a plant is stressed at all is very reliable. Naming the exact stress is harder, almost entirely because low light and shade look alike: both mean less light.
