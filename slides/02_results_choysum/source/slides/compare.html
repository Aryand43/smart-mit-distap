Four conditions, chance 0.25. Leave one plant out, white-light normalised. One locked setting for both patch models. Bold = best in column

All five models are tested the same way: train on two plants, test on the unseen third, each plant in turn. PLS-DA and SVM are deterministic (3 runs); gradient boosting and the two patch models use 5 seeds per test plant (15 runs). The patch setting (32 px, stride 2, plus learning rate, optimiser and batch size) was picked automatically from the GPU tuning run as the single best combination over all trials. With that one setting, ResNet-50 gives 0.69, against 0.75 when each test plant gets its own setting; both should be reported. The ViT has the best median, but its mean and F1 equal ResNet-50 and gradient boosting, so with three plants the top four are within noise. Every patch model stopped training after 1 epoch, a sign the model learns plant-specific details quickly.
