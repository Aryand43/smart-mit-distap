Earliest detection

ResNet-50 reaches 0.92 at 4 hours, the ViT 0.90

All five models are tested the same way: train on two plants, test on the unseen third, each plant in turn. Classic models: each training image gives 5,000 random plant pixels, repeated for 20 draws that cover different regions; each test plant is labelled by the vote of its pixels (60 runs). Deep models: 5 runs per test plant (15 runs). PLS-DA on pixels was already run (0.62); the SVM, gradient boosting and the vision transformer are running on the GPU now, and the slide is filled in when they finish.
