Light conditions

Handling white-light and low-light images

Calibrate colour

A colour chart in every frame plus a quadratic RGB correction kept canopy colour consistent across months of changing light (Chopin et al., 2018).

Enhance dark images

EnlightenGAN low-light enhancement before YOLOv8 raised disease-severity mAP by 2.47 points (Ye et al., 2024).

Use robust colour indices

Normalised chromatic indices such as ExG separated plants under both shaded and sunlit conditions (Woebbecke et al., 1995).

Light as the stress

Raman detected light-saturation stress, among others, within 48 h (Altangerel et al., 2017). RGB evidence for light stress is thin.

Uncontrolled illumination is a key challenge for visible-range methods: Barbedo, 2016

Two separate questions: (1) the light condition as an imaging nuisance, handled by calibration and enhancement; (2) light as the stress itself. For (2), I found spectral evidence but no verified RGB deep-learning paper. That is a gap our experiments could fill.
