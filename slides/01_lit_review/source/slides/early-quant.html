RGB is used more here than for early detection, because severity and freshness are visible

Beyond yes-or-no detection, these papers estimate how severe the stress is, how long it has lasted, or how long produce will stay sellable. Das Choudhury 2023 is closest to our time-point goal: it predicts the day stress begins. Shelf-life work shows the same methods apply after harvest.
