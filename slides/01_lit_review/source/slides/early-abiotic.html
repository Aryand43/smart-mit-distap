Teo et al. (2024) studied the same crop as our first dataset, choy sum

Abiotic and nutrient stress is detected later than disease, at around 2 to 4 days at the earliest. Teo 2024 is directly relevant: choy sum, hyperspectral, nutrient deficiency detected at day 4 without visible symptoms. Weksler 2021 imaged hourly but did not report an hour-level detection time.
