Fundamentals

The core methods in plain terms

MethodWhat it doesWhy it matters for us
Features + classical MLPeople choose features (colour indices, texture); a classifier such as SVM or PLS-DA separates the classesSimple baseline that works with little data
CNNLearns features such as edges, colour changes and lesions directly from the image, then outputs a classTakes any 3-channel image, so it fits our three-band images
Transfer learningStarts from a CNN already trained on millions of everyday photos, then fine-tunes it on plant imagesOur lab datasets are small
LSTMReads a sequence one step at a time and remembers what came beforeOur images are taken at several time points
Hybrid CNN-LSTMThe CNN turns each time point's image into features; the LSTM links them across timeMain model; beat a CNN alone, 76.8% to 93% (Taghavi Namin et al., 2018)
Vision transformer (ViT)Splits the image into patches and uses attention to weigh how each patch relates to the othersCan be combined with a CNN as a hybrid to explore
Encoder-decoderThe encoder compresses the input into a compact form; the decoder builds an output from it, such as a maskTo explore, e.g. for segmenting plants or learning features
These are the building blocks behind the papers in the review and behind the plan we discussed: a hybrid CNN-LSTM on three-band image sequences, with encoder-decoder models as an option to explore.
