Gaps

Where our experiments can add something new

Hour-level time points

No paper found detects drought, salt or nutrient stress within hours; the earliest is 3 to 4 days, and none with RGB.

White vs low light

Few studies compare the same model on white-light and low-light images of the same plants.

Which bands to use

Most reviewed papers use plain RGB. Whether three index-selected bands work better is open for our data.

Small lab datasets

Controlled experiments give few images, so transfer learning and augmentation are needed (Barbedo, 2018).

These gaps come from this review pass, not an exhaustive search. Hour-level time-point classification from RGB is the strongest candidate for novelty, and fits the 4-hour stress experiments.
