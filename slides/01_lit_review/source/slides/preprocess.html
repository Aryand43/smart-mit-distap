For our white-light vs low-light data, the two most relevant steps are colour calibration with a colour chart (Chopin 2018) and low-light enhancement (Ye 2024). ExG segmentation is cheap and a good first step for background removal. On Mohanty 2016: colour images beat grayscale; segmented images were also tested.
