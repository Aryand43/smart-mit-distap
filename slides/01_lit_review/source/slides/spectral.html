PLS-DA appears often; no PLSR-specific stress paper was verified in this pass

Spectral methods catch stress earlier than RGB because they see chemistry (pigments, water, nitrate) before visible symptoms. They are a useful benchmark: can RGB plus deep learning match their early-detection timing? Gupta 2020 lists crops Arabidopsis, Pak Choi and Choy Sum.
