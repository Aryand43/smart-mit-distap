No paper found detects abiotic stress within hours, and no RGB paper reports a pre-symptomatic detection time

A targeted search for early-detection work, as Prof. Ram asked. The earliest results come from spectral sensors on disease. Drought, salt and nutrient stress are detected later, at around 2 to 4 days. RGB papers mostly classify severity or freshness rather than detect early. Our 4-hour data sits in the window nobody has covered.
