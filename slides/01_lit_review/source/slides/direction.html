Agreed direction

Three-band inputs and hybrid CNN models

Input data

  • A new index identifies three regions; one band is taken from each to build a three-band image
  • Received: choy sum under high, low and white light, at 4 h and days 1 to 3
  • Next: kailan, then the full dataset (including Arabidopsis) within 1 to 2 weeks

Model

  • Hybrid models: CNN-LSTM as the main model, plus CNN with vision transformer
  • Train and tune each crop separately, then on crops combined
  • Also to explore: encoder-decoder architectures
Outcome of the discussions with Dr Praveen. The CNN-LSTM links time points of the same plant; the CNN-vision transformer hybrid adds attention. Results are reported per crop first, then with crops merged.
