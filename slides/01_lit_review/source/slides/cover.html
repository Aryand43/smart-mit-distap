Methods for image classification of plant stress

ML/DL techniques and image preprocessing for disease, stress, early-detection and time-point classification

Prepared by

Aryan Dutt, Visiting Student

Supervisors

Prof. Rajeev Ram
Dr Praveen Kumar Jayapal

Literature review of methods for image classification and image preprocessing for plant stress analysis, covering RGB, three-band and hyperspectral imaging. Stress is treated broadly (biotic and abiotic), with a focus on early detection, light conditions and time-point analysis.
