Overview

Most studies follow the same five-step pipeline

1

Acquire

RGB or multi-band images, taken at one or several time points

2

Preprocess

Colour correction, background removal, resizing, augmentation

3

Represent

Hand-made colour and texture features, or features learned by a CNN

4

Model

Classical ML, CNN, CNN-LSTM for sequences, encoder-decoder

5

Output

Class (healthy, diseased, stressed), severity score or time point

Synthesis of the reviewed papers

Steps 2 to 4 are where the literature differs most. Deep learning merges steps 3 and 4, but preprocessing still matters, especially for images taken under changing light.
